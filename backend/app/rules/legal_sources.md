# DRISHTI Authoritative Legal Rule Source Inventory

## Governing Framework
- **Primary Act**: The Legal Metrology Act, 2009
- **Primary Rules**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (LMPC Rules)

---

## 1. LMPC Rules, 2011 (Base Version)
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 202(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2011-03-07
- **Commencement Date**: 2011-04-01
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Rule 6 (Mandatory Declarations), Rule 10, Rule 11, Rule 18
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Replaced the Standards of Weights and Measures (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 1977
- **Official Source URL**: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: MRP, NET_QUANTITY, MANUFACTURER_PACKER_IMPORTER, MANUFACTURE_DATE/PACKING_DATE/IMPORT_DATE, CONSUMER_CARE
- **Status**: HISTORICAL_VERSION
- **Description**: Establishes the baseline mandatory declarations for retail packages.

---

## 2. LMPC (Amendment) Rules, 2015
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2015
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 385(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2015-05-14
- **Commencement Date**: 2016-01-01
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Rule 6(2) [Consumer Care]
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Substituted Rule 6(2).
- **Official Source URL**: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: CONSUMER_CARE
- **Status**: CURRENTLY_RELEVANT
- **Description**: Mandated the consumer-complaint contact declaration to include name/address, telephone number, and e-mail address.

---

## 3. LMPC (Amendment) Rules, 2017
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2017
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 629(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2017-06-23
- **Commencement Date**: 2018-01-01
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Definitions, Rule 6, MRP, Dates, Manufacturer/Address, display/letter-size provisions
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Extensive amendments to Rule 6 and formatting rules.
- **Official Source URL**: https://egazette.nic.in/
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: MRP, MANUFACTURER_PACKER_IMPORTER, Dates
- **Status**: CURRENTLY_RELEVANT (subject to later amendments)
- **Description**: Expanded definitions, updated display and letter-size provisions, modified date formatting rules, updated manufacturer/address requirements, and explicitly banned dual MRPs (no person shall declare different MRPs on identical pre-packaged commodities).

---

## 4. LMPC (Amendment) Rules, 2021 & 2022 Chain
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2021
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 779(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2021-11-02
- **Commencement Date**: Postponed to 2022-10-01 (by G.S.R. 226(E))

- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2022
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 226(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2022-03-28
- **Commencement Date**: 2022-10-01
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Rule 6(1)(e) [MRP], Rule 6(1)(c) [Net Quantity], Rule 6(11) [Unit Sale Price]
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Substituted the unit-sale-price provision and changed the commencement date of the 2021 amendment.
- **Official Source URL**: https://egazette.nic.in/
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: MRP, NET_QUANTITY
- **Status**: CURRENTLY_RELEVANT
- **Description**: Mandates MRP in Indian currency inclusive of all taxes, removed Schedule II (standard pack sizes), and substituted the Unit Sale Price declaration requirement.

## 5. LMPC (Second Amendment) Rules, 2022 (Electronic QR)
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Second Amendment) Rules, 2022
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 514(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2022-07-14
- **Commencement Date**: 2022-07-14
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Rule 6 (Proviso)
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Inserts proviso for electronic products.
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: All
- **Status**: OUT_OF_CURRENT_DRISHTI_SCOPE
- **Description**: Allows electronic products to use QR codes for mandatory declarations (except MRP). Out of scope for physical-label extraction.

## 8. LMPC (Amendment) Rules, 2025 (Medical Devices)
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules, 2025
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 778(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2025-10-24
- **Commencement Date**: 2025-10-24
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Exemption provisions (Rule 33 / PDP declarations)
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Exempts packages containing medical devices from specific LMPC labeling rules, including font-size and dimensional standards, provided they comply with the Medical Devices Rules, 2017.
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: None directly for generic inspection (may matter later for DRISHTI font/letter-size inspection).
- **Affected Product Category**: Medical Devices
- **Status**: SECTOR_SPECIFIC, OUT_OF_CURRENT_GENERIC_DRISHTI_SCOPE
- **Description**: Harmonizes labeling requirements by stipulating that medical devices complying with the Medical Devices Rules, 2017 are not required to strictly follow LMPC font sizes, dimension standards, or Principal Display Panel constraints. Medical-device-specific requirements should not be applied to ordinary packaged commodities.

---

## 9. LMPC Third Amendment Rules, 2026
- **Official Title**: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Third Amendment Rules, 2026
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 418(E)
- **Publication Date**: 2026-05-29
- **Commencement Date**: 2026-05-29
- **Rule/Sub-rule Affected**: Rule 4, Rule 27
- **Modifies/Replaces**: Amends labeling context for bonded warehouses and registration requirements.
- **Affected DRISHTI Fields**: None
- **Status**: CONTEXTUAL, OUT_OF_CURRENT_DRISHTI_RULE_EVALUATION_SCOPE
- **Description**: Permits importers to make mandatory declarations on retail packages at bonded warehouses operated by AEO Tier-2 and Tier-3 certified entities, provided labeling is completed before the goods leave the warehouse (Rule 4 context). Also mandates corporate accountability disclosures and indefinite registration validity updates (Rule 27 changes). These do not alter current DRISHTI declaration-family checks for an ordinary physical-package inspection.
